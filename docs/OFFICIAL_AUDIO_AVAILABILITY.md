## Authorized acquisition routes

- [Sony Music Publishing game-use application](https://www.smpj.jp/wp-content/themes/smp/share/pdf/songs/game.pdf): application and written agreement process, not a general license or master download. Confirm which publisher and master owner controls each selected release.
- [Nippon Columbia recording-use applications](https://columbia.jp/legal/): permission request for Columbia masters; purchasing the recording does not supply game rights.
- [Avex music and recording permissions](https://avexmusicpublishing.com/contact/): composition and recording permissions are separate. Use the actual release's holder, not an assumed franchise-wide owner.
- [Spotify policy](https://developer.spotify.com/policy): game integration and synchronization prohibited; not an eligible automatic battle playback route.
- [SoundCloud API terms](https://developers.soundcloud.com/docs/api/terms-of-use): games require all relevant licenses, including synchronization; an official artist upload alone does not grant those rights.
- [YouTube player policy](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies): hidden/background players and isolating audio from video are prohibited. Official uploads are references, not audio-only game masters.
- [Apple Music user authorization](https://developer.apple.com/musickit/): requires the visitor's authorization, so does not meet setup-free visitor playback; no game master/synchronization grant was established. Retail Qobuz/Apple/OTOTOY catalog listings likewise do not grant public hosting.

No license requests, purchases, uploads, push or deployment were performed. No service credentials or visitor music subscriptions were added.
