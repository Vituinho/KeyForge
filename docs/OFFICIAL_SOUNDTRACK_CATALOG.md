# Official soundtrack reference catalog

Reviewed: 2026-10-19. All 56 encounters have a catalog record: **22 verified references, 26 unresolved references, 8 KeyForge originals**. Of the unresolved references, 22 have verified individual titles but incomplete individual composition credits; 4 still need a suitable individual cue.

This is a metadata catalog, not an installed official music inventory. **Zero official recordings are installed or authorized for game playback.** The 56 bundled original defaults remain the actual runtime tracks. Listening subscriptions, purchase links, previews and playlist inclusion grant no game synchronization or redistribution rights. No streaming audio was extracted, downloaded or embedded.

Machine-readable records: [soundtrackReferences.json](../src/lib/audio/soundtrackReferences.json). Actual playable manifest: [SOUNDTRACK_MANIFEST.md](SOUNDTRACK_MANIFEST.md). The catalog is not imported by the player or hook.

## Verification rules

- Verified: individual title appears in a primary catalog; its release identifies a score creator. Credits marked **score** identify the release score composer, not a claim of per-track liner-note inspection. Credits marked **track** are supported by explicit publisher track-range composition credits. Dragon Ball opening/ending songs can have different composers and are excluded from those score selections.
- Unresolved: title, individual composition credit or a suitable encounter selection could not be established. **Release music credits are not silently converted into sole track authorship.** Credited recording artists are preserved separately from composers.
- Original: newly authored procedural composition and synthetic recording, with generator credit rather than an invented human composer. Rights and hashes accompany the bundled files.
- Assignments are KeyForge editorial choices unless the track title or publisher announcement names the character/fight. A verified recording does not prove scene usage, exclusivity or an official endorsement of KeyForge. Solo Leveling later encounters intentionally reuse first-season score selections; no unreleased character themes are claimed.
- Japanese publisher titles are retained exactly, rather than fan translations or invented English cue names. DARK ARIA is the album track; DARK ARIA <LV2> is not treated as interchangeable.

The supplied Spotify pages identify the releases but their normal HTML did not expose tracklists. Public Spotify embed pages were read for **metadata only**; no embed, SDK, preview or playback integration was added. The One Piece reference is specifically GEAR5, not a complete series catalog. It supports Sanji, Zoro, Law, Kaido and Luffy selections; it does not establish a theme for Usopp, Doflamingo or Katakuri.

Nippon Columbia confirms Dragon Ball Z Music Collection Vol.1 (COCC-72061) and Shunsuke Kikuchi score credits, but the accessible official page does not list individual instrumental cues. Its listed theme-song references must not be attributed to Kikuchi solely from album score credits. Instead, all eight Dragon Ball encounters use individually verified Super or Super: Broly score selections from Columbia/Avex. Vegeta, Frieza, Broly and Goku have named character cues. Krillin, Piccolo, Cell and Gohan use explicit editorial selections rather than invented exclusive themes. Columbia also provides precise composition track ranges for the 2016 score; these are recorded as track-level credits. The Broly composer is corroborated by his direct interview. Kokushibo likewise remains unresolved in the supplied Demon Slayer releases.

To clear an official replacement, obtain the exact master recording, composition/synchronization and master-use/distribution permission applicable to this game, territory and hosting method; document rights and attribution, supply a real asset (or expressly approved playback integration), then update the playable manifest and run the strict asset audit. A verified reference status never grants playback permission. No backend, authentication, gameplay or deployment configuration changes were made for this catalog.
