# Character battle soundtrack manifest

Generated by `npm run audit:audio`. **0/56 assets missing; 0/56 lack licensing evidence.**

All encounters ship with original synthetic battle loops, enabled by default. No official franchise recordings are included. Musical directions below guide encounter mood, not claims of official music. Reproduce the recordings with `npx tsx scripts/generate-battle-audio.ts`. The original-audio permission notice is in `public/audio/bgm/original/LICENSE.txt`; the full generator and SHA-256 provenance accompany the recordings. The audit verifies the original recordings against their provenance, PCM structure and non-silent/non-clipped peaks.

Each enemy remains independently configurable in `src/lib/audio/battleMusic.ts`. Official replacement recordings require supplied distribution rights covering the master and composition, actual files, source/creator, licensing evidence, commercial-use permission and attribution before enabling. Personal local overrides remain browser-only and do not satisfy this distribution audit. No global fallback is configured.
